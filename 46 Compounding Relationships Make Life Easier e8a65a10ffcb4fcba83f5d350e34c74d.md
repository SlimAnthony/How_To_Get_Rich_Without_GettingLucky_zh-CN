# **复利式的人际关系让生活更轻松**

## 复利不仅发生在金钱上，也发生在人际关系中

**Naval：** 人生中最大的回报都来自复利，而复利并不只存在于资本之中——它同样存在于人际关系里。

The biggest returns in life come from compound interest, and compound interest doesn't just apply to capital — it applies to relationships, too.

那些在你生命中存在时间最长、彼此信任最深的关系，正是回报最高的关系。信任是一点一滴积累起来的：合作的时间越长，彼此的了解越深，信任的"本金"就越大，它带来的回报也会像利滚利一样越来越大。

The relationships that have been in your life the longest, where the trust runs deepest, are the ones that pay the highest returns. Trust accumulates bit by bit: the longer you work together and the better you know each other, the larger the principal of trust becomes — and the returns compound on top of it.

## 成熟的关系省去了无穷的摩擦成本

和一个共事了十年、二十年的人合作，你们不需要冗长的合同，不需要反复的谈判，也不需要互相提防。一句话就能说清的事情，不必写十页备忘录。**你们已经为彼此的可靠性付过学费了，之后的每一次合作都在享受这笔投资的利息。**

When you work with someone you've known for ten or twenty years, you don't need long contracts, endless negotiations, or constant vigilance. Things that take one sentence to communicate don't need a ten-page memo. You've already paid the tuition to learn each other's reliability — every collaboration after that is collecting interest on the investment.

相反，每开始一段新的合作关系，你都要从零开始建立信任——尽职调查、试探、磨合。这些摩擦成本是巨大的，而且每换一个人就要重新支付一次。

By contrast, every new relationship means building trust from scratch — due diligence, testing, adjustment. Those friction costs are enormous, and you pay them all over again with every new person.

## 尽早剔除坏的关系，让好的关系长期复利

这也意味着两件事。第一，要尽早识别并退出那些消耗你的坏关系——不诚信的合伙人、负和博弈的圈子。在坏关系上停留的每一年，都是在为负复利买单。

This implies two things. First, identify and exit bad relationships early — dishonest partners, negative-sum circles. Every year spent in a bad relationship is paying negative compound interest.

第二，对于那些经受住时间考验的好关系，要长期持有、持续投入，就像对待最优质的资产一样。随着年龄增长，你会发现人生中最重要的财富之一，就是一小群你可以无条件信任、可以随时并肩作战的老朋友和老伙伴。

Second, hold on to the good relationships that have stood the test of time and keep investing in them, the way you'd treat your highest-quality assets. As you get older, you'll find that one of the greatest forms of wealth is a small group of old friends and partners you can trust unconditionally and go to battle with at any time.

**人生的下半场，几乎所有重要的机会，都来自这些复利式的关系。**

In the second half of life, almost every important opportunity comes through these compounding relationships.
