# **附赠篇：挤出时间投资自己**

## 时间不是"找"出来的，而是"腾"出来的

**Nivi：** 很多人说：我也想投资自己，但我实在没有时间。

**Naval：** 没有人会凭空"找到"时间。时间从来不是找出来的，而是腾出来的——你必须主动把它从别的事情那里夺回来。

Nobody ever "finds" time. Time is never found; it's made — you have to actively take it back from other things.

说自己没时间，其实是在说：它的优先级不够高。如果你真的相信投资自己是回报最高的事情，你自然会把它排在日程表的最前面，而不是塞在所有杂事之后。

Saying you don't have time really means it's not a high enough priority. If you truly believe investing in yourself has the highest return, you'll put it at the front of your calendar, not squeeze it in after all the errands.

## 用你的目标时薪，买回你的时间

还记得"给自己设定一个有抱负的时薪"吗？这里是它最实际的应用。任何可以用低于你目标时薪的价格外包出去的事情，都应该外包出去——请人打扫、送货上门、花钱省事。**用金钱买回时间，永远是划算的交易，因为金钱可以再生，而时间不能。**

Remember setting an aspirational hourly rate for yourself? This is its most practical application. Anything you can outsource for less than your aspirational rate, you should outsource — hire the cleaner, get things delivered, pay to save the hassle. Buying back time with money is always a good trade, because you can always make more money, but you cannot make more time.

同样地，对不重要的会议、不必要的社交、消耗性的娱乐说"不"。你的日程表上的每一个空档，都是可以用来复利增长的资本。

Likewise, say no to unimportant meetings, unnecessary socializing, and draining entertainment. Every open slot on your calendar is capital that can compound.

## 把腾出来的时间，投入会复利的事情

腾出时间之后，把它投到那些会复利的事情上：读你热爱的书，学习基础学科，打磨你的专长知识，锻炼身体，冥想静心，和值得长期交往的人建立关系。

Once you've made the time, put it into the things that compound: read what you love, learn the fundamentals, sharpen your specific knowledge, train your body, calm your mind, and build relationships with people worth knowing for decades.

这些投资短期内看不到回报，这正是大多数人放弃它们的原因——也正是坚持它们的人最终与众不同的原因。复利的曲线，前半段总是平得让人绝望，后半段却陡得让人震惊。

These investments show no return in the short term — which is exactly why most people give up on them, and exactly why the people who stick with them end up different. The compounding curve is always desperately flat in the first half and shockingly steep in the second.

**把自己当作人生中最重要的项目来投资。其他一切，都只是这项投资的利息。**

Treat yourself as the most important project of your life. Everything else is just interest on that investment.
