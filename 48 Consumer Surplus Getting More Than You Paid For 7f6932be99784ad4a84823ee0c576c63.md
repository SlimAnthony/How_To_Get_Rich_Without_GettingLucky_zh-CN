***People are willing to pay more than what companies charge***

# **消费者剩余：得到的比付出的多**

## 人们愿意支付的价格，往往高于公司实际收取的价格

**Naval：** 消费者剩余（consumer surplus）是与价格歧视相对应的另一个微观经济学概念。它指的是：你愿意为一件商品支付的最高价格，与你实际支付的价格之间的差额。这部分差额，就是你作为消费者白白赚到的价值。

Consumer surplus is the microeconomic concept on the flip side of price discrimination. It's the difference between the maximum you would have been willing to pay for something and what you actually paid. That difference is value you capture for free as a consumer.

假设你口渴难耐，愿意为一瓶水付10美元，但商店只收1美元——那么你就获得了9美元的消费者剩余。交易结束时，你得到的价值超过了你的付出。

Say you're dying of thirst and would happily pay $10 for a bottle of water, but the store charges $1 — you just captured $9 of consumer surplus. You walked away from the transaction with more value than you gave up.

## 科技产品创造了史上最庞大的消费者剩余

现代科技公司创造了人类历史上最庞大的消费者剩余。想一想：谷歌搜索、地图、维基百科、开源软件——你为它们支付了多少钱？几乎为零。但如果它们明天消失，你愿意付多少钱把它们买回来？对很多人来说，这个数字大得惊人。

Modern tech companies have created the largest consumer surplus in human history. Think about it: Google search, maps, Wikipedia, open-source software — how much do you pay for them? Almost nothing. But if they disappeared tomorrow, how much would you pay to get them back? For most people, a staggering amount.

这也是为什么GDP这类统计数字会系统性地低估科技带来的真实财富增长——**GDP只统计成交的价格，而不统计人们免费获得的价值。**一部智能手机里免费装着的地图、相机、百科全书和通讯工具，在几十年前价值数万美元，如今却几乎不计入任何经济统计。

This is also why statistics like GDP systematically understate the real growth in wealth that technology delivers — GDP measures prices paid, not value received for free. The maps, camera, encyclopedia, and communication tools bundled free into a smartphone would have cost tens of thousands of dollars a few decades ago, yet today they barely register in any economic statistic.

## 创造大量消费者剩余的生意，才是最好的生意

对创业者来说，这里有一个深刻的启示：最伟大的公司，恰恰是那些创造了远超其收费的价值的公司。你捕获的价值只是你创造的价值的一小部分——而这是好事。

For entrepreneurs, there's a deep lesson here: the greatest companies are precisely the ones that create far more value than they charge for. The value you capture is only a fraction of the value you create — and that's a good thing.

**给用户留下大量的消费者剩余，用户就会替你传播、替你辩护、对你忠诚。**想从每一笔交易中榨干最后一分钱的公司，反而走不远。这与"为世界创造富足"是同一个道理：先让社会变得更富有，社会才会回报你。

Leave your users a large consumer surplus, and they'll spread the word for you, defend you, and stay loyal. Companies that try to squeeze the last cent out of every transaction don't go far. It's the same principle as making abundance for the world: enrich society first, and society will reward you.
