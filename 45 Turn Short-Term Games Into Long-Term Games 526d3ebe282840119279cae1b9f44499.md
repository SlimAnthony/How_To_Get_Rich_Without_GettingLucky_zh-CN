# **把短期博弈变成长期博弈**

## 单次博弈中，背叛往往是"理性"的选择

**Nivi：** 我们之前谈过"和长期主义者玩长期游戏"。这一讲，我们来聊聊如何把短期博弈变成长期博弈。

**Naval：** 博弈论中最经典的模型是"囚徒困境"（prisoner's dilemma）。在一次性的囚徒困境中，双方都有强烈的动机去背叛对方——因为无论对方怎么选，背叛都能让自己的处境更好。于是两个"理性"的人最终都选择背叛，得到一个对双方都更糟的结果。

The classic model in game theory is the prisoner's dilemma. In a one-shot prisoner's dilemma, both sides have a strong incentive to defect — no matter what the other person does, defecting leaves you better off. So two "rational" people both end up defecting, and both end up worse off than if they had cooperated.

这就是短期博弈的本质问题：当双方都认定这是最后一次交易、以后再无往来时，欺骗、压榨、占便宜就成了占优策略。

That's the fundamental problem with short-term games: when both sides believe this is the last transaction and they'll never deal with each other again, cheating, squeezing, and free-riding become the dominant strategies.

## 重复博弈让合作成为最优策略

但只要把同一个博弈重复进行下去——变成所谓的"重复囚徒困境"（iterated prisoner's dilemma）——局面就完全不同了。当你知道下一轮还要和同一个人打交道时，这一轮的背叛就会在下一轮遭到报复。

But if you repeat the same game over and over — the so-called iterated prisoner's dilemma — everything changes. When you know you'll be dealing with the same person in the next round, defecting this round invites retaliation in the next.

在重复博弈的计算机竞赛中，胜出的往往是最简单的策略——"以牙还牙"（tit-for-tat）：先合作，之后对方怎么对你，你就怎么对对方。合作会自发地从重复中涌现出来，不需要任何中央权威来强制执行。

In computer tournaments of iterated games, the winning strategy tends to be the simplest one — tit-for-tat: cooperate first, then do to the other player whatever they did to you last round. Cooperation emerges spontaneously out of repetition, with no central authority needed to enforce it.

**换句话说，博弈的次数本身，就是诚信的执行机制。**

In other words, the repetition of the game itself is the enforcement mechanism for honest behavior.

## 如何把短期博弈变成长期博弈

那么，怎样才能把短期博弈变成长期博弈？

So how do you turn a short-term game into a long-term game?

首先是让重复变得可预期：与同样的人长期共事，深耕同一个行业、同一个圈子。当所有人都预期未来还会反复相遇时，每个人自然会约束自己的行为。

First, make repetition predictable: work with the same people over long periods, and stay in the same industry and the same circles. When everyone expects to run into each other again and again, people naturally police their own behavior.

其次是引入声誉机制。声誉的作用，是把你和一个人的单次博弈，变成你和整个社会的重复博弈——你在一笔交易中欺骗了一个人，等于向所有未来的交易对手宣告了你的不可信。

Second, bring reputation into the game. What reputation does is convert your one-shot game with a single person into a repeated game with all of society — cheat one person in one deal, and you've announced your untrustworthiness to every future counterparty.

这也是为什么互联网时代的声誉如此值钱：你的历史记录公开可查，跟随你一生。

This is also why reputation is so valuable in the internet age: your track record is public, searchable, and follows you for life.

## 长期博弈是正和博弈：大家一起把蛋糕做大

短期博弈是零和的：蛋糕就这么大，人人都在抢着切分它。而长期博弈是正和的：**在长期博弈中，大家是在互相成就、一起把蛋糕做大；在短期博弈中，大家只是在瓜分彼此。**

Short-term games are zero-sum: the pie is fixed, and everyone is fighting to carve it up. Long-term games are positive-sum: in a long-term game, everyone is making each other rich and baking the pie together; in a short-term game, everyone is just carving each other up.

人生中所有真正的回报——无论是财富、人际关系还是知识——都来自复利。而复利只会发生在长期博弈里。所以，尽可能把你参与的每一场短期博弈，都变成长期博弈。

All the real returns in life — whether wealth, relationships, or knowledge — come from compound interest. And compound interest only happens in long-term games. So whenever possible, turn every short-term game you're in into a long-term one.
