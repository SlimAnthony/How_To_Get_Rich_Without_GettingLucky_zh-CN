# **谢林点：无需沟通即可协作**

## 谢林点是博弈论中，人们无需沟通也能达成一致的答案

**Nivi：** 我们来聊聊谢林点（Schelling point）。

**Naval：** 谢林点，又称聚焦点（focal point），是博弈论中的一个概念，由经济学家托马斯·谢林（Thomas Schelling）在他的著作《冲突的策略》（*The Strategy of Conflict*）中提出。

A Schelling point, or focal point, is a game theory concept coined by the economist Thomas Schelling in his book *The Strategy of Conflict*.

它指的是：在无法沟通的情况下，人们仍然会不约而同地收敛到某个特定的答案上——因为这个答案在某种意义上显得"特殊"或"突出"，即便它本身并不比其他选项更"正确"。

It's the solution people tend to arrive at in the absence of communication, because it seems natural, special, or relevant to them — even though it isn't objectively any more "correct" than the alternatives.

## 经典案例：如何在无法沟通的情况下，在纽约见面

谢林最初举的例子是这样的：假设你需要在纽约市与另一个人见面，但你们完全无法提前沟通任何细节——没有约定的时间，也没有约定的地点。你会选在哪里、什么时候？

Schelling's original example goes like this: Say you need to meet another person somewhere in New York City, but you can't communicate any details beforehand — no agreed time, no agreed place. Where and when would you go?

结果显示，绝大多数人给出的答案惊人地一致：中央车站（Grand Central Terminal），中午十二点。这个地点和这个时间，并没有什么客观上"正确"的理由——只是因为它们在大多数人心目中显得格外醒目、格外"经典"，所以大家不约而同地选择了它。

The results show that an overwhelming majority of people converge on the same answer: Grand Central Terminal, at noon. There's nothing objectively "correct" about that place or that time — it's simply the choice that stands out as the obvious, iconic one, so people independently converge on it.

## 谢林点让人们无需沟通、无需签订合同，也能彼此协作

Naval认为，谢林点的意义远不止是一个有趣的思想实验——它解释了人类社会中大量隐性协作是如何发生的。

Naval's take is that Schelling points are more than an interesting thought experiment — they explain a huge amount of the implicit coordination that happens throughout human society.

举例来说，在商业谈判中，双方常常会不约而同地锚定在某个整数、某个"对半分"的方案，或某个约定俗成的惯例上——不是因为这是数学上最优的分配方式，而是因为它是双方都能轻易识别、并且相信对方也会认同的"焦点"。**这让人们即便缺乏信任、无法签订正式合同，也能够达成协议、彼此协作。**

For example, in a business negotiation, both sides will often independently anchor on a round number, a 50/50 split, or some other customary convention — not because it's the mathematically optimal allocation, but because it's a focal point both sides can easily recognize and expect the other to recognize as well. This lets people reach agreements and cooperate even when trust is scarce and a formal contract isn't possible.

## 社会规范、法律乃至货币，都是被固化下来的谢林点

法律、社会规范，甚至货币本身，某种程度上都是被固化下来的谢林点——它们是整个社会共同收敛于其上的协调机制，使得原本互不相识、无法沟通的人们，也能够形成稳定的预期，并据此行动。

Laws, social norms, and even money itself are, in a sense, codified Schelling points — coordination mechanisms an entire society converges on, letting people who don't know each other and can't communicate form stable expectations and act on them.

理解谢林点，可以帮助你在商业和生活中找到那些"显而易见"的答案——那些即便没有人明说，大家也会不约而同选择的方案，从而更高效地达成共识、减少摩擦。

Understanding Schelling points can help you find the "obvious" answers in business and in life — the solutions people converge on even when nobody spells them out — so you can reach agreement and reduce friction more efficiently.
