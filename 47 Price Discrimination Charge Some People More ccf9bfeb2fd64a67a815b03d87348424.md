# **价格歧视：向某些人收更高的价**

## 同一件商品，可以卖出不同的价格

**Naval：** 价格歧视（price discrimination）是微观经济学里一个非常重要、却常被误解的概念。它的意思是：对同一件产品或服务，向不同的顾客收取不同的价格——依据的不是成本差异，而是各人支付意愿（willingness to pay）的差异。

Price discrimination is a very important and often misunderstood concept in microeconomics. It means charging different customers different prices for the same product or service — based not on differences in cost, but on differences in each person's willingness to pay.

"歧视"这个词听起来刺耳，但这里它并不带有道德贬义——它只是一个描述定价策略的技术术语。

The word "discrimination" sounds harsh, but there's no moral judgment implied here — it's simply a technical term describing a pricing strategy.

## 价格歧视无处不在

一旦你理解了这个概念，就会发现它无处不在。航空公司的头等舱和经济舱，运送的是同一班飞机上的乘客，价格却相差十倍——头等舱的富裕乘客实际上补贴了后舱的机票。软件公司的"企业版"和"个人版"，功能相差无几，价格却天壤之别。电影院的学生票和老人票、软件的教育折扣、丰俭由人的菜单，本质上都是价格歧视。

Once you understand the concept, you see it everywhere. First class and economy on an airline move passengers on the same plane at prices that differ tenfold — the wealthy passengers up front are effectively subsidizing the tickets in the back. A software company's "enterprise" and "personal" tiers differ little in functionality but enormously in price. Student and senior discounts at the movies, education pricing for software, menus with cheap and lavish options — all of it is price discrimination.

## 向付得起的人收更高的价，让付不起的人也能用上

价格歧视之所以强大，是因为任何市场里，不同顾客愿意支付的价格天然就不同。如果你只定一个价：定高了，就把大量价格敏感的顾客拒之门外；定低了，就把富裕顾客本来愿意付的钱白白留在了桌上。

Price discrimination is powerful because in any market, different customers are naturally willing to pay different amounts. If you charge a single price, you face a dilemma: set it high and you shut out all the price-sensitive customers; set it low and you leave money on the table that wealthier customers were happy to pay.

通过区分定价，你可以同时服务两端：**向支付意愿高的人收更高的价格，从而捕获更多价值；同时让支付意愿低的人也能以低价用上你的产品，扩大你的市场。**做得好的价格歧视，甚至是双赢的——穷学生用上了打折的软件，企业客户为高级支持付了高价，而产品得以覆盖所有人。

By segmenting prices, you can serve both ends at once: charge more to those with high willingness to pay, capturing more value, while letting those with low willingness to pay access your product cheaply, expanding your market. Done well, price discrimination is even win-win — the broke student gets discounted software, the enterprise customer pays up for premium support, and the product reaches everybody.

如果你在经营生意，就应该认真思考如何对你的产品进行版本划分和差异化定价。这是提高利润最直接的杠杆之一。

If you're running a business, you should think hard about how to version your product and price it differentially. It's one of the most direct levers for increasing your margins.
