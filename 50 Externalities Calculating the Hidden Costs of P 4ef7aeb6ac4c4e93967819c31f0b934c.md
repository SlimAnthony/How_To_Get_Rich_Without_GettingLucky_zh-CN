# **外部性：计算产品的隐性成本**

## 有些成本和收益，没有被计入价格

**Naval：** 外部性（externality）是指一笔交易对交易之外的第三方造成的影响——这种影响没有反映在价格里，也没有人为它付钱或收钱。

An externality is the effect a transaction has on a third party outside the transaction — an effect that isn't reflected in the price, that nobody pays for or gets paid for.

最经典的例子是污染。一家工厂生产商品、卖给顾客，双方各取所需。但工厂排出的废气废水，伤害的是下游的居民——他们既不是买家也不是卖家，却在替这笔交易埋单。产品的标价里，没有包含这部分强加给社会的成本。

The classic example is pollution. A factory makes goods and sells them to customers, and both sides get what they want. But the fumes and waste the factory dumps hurt the people downstream — people who are neither buyers nor sellers, yet who are footing part of the bill for the transaction. The price on the product doesn't include the cost imposed on society.

这就是负外部性（negative externality）。也存在正外部性（positive externality）：养蜂人的蜜蜂顺便为邻居的果园授了粉；你接种疫苗，也保护了你周围的人；一个人受了良好的教育，整个社会都会受益。

That's a negative externality. Positive externalities exist too: a beekeeper's bees pollinate the neighbor's orchard for free; when you get vaccinated, you also protect the people around you; when one person gets a good education, all of society benefits.

## 有外部性存在时，市场价格就是失真的

外部性之所以重要，是因为它意味着市场价格失真了。当负外部性没有被计价时，那种产品就会被过度生产——因为生产者没有承担全部成本；当正外部性没有被计价时，那种产品就会供给不足——因为生产者没有获得全部回报。

Externalities matter because they mean market prices are distorted. When a negative externality goes unpriced, that product gets overproduced — because the producer isn't bearing the full cost. When a positive externality goes unpriced, that product gets underproduced — because the producer isn't capturing the full benefit.

经济学家对此的标准解法，是设法"把外部性内部化"（internalize the externality）：让隐性成本变成显性价格。对污染征税（比如碳税），让排放者为每一吨排放付费；对教育、科研、疫苗提供补贴，让正外部性的创造者得到回报。

The standard economic answer is to internalize the externality: turn the hidden cost into an explicit price. Tax pollution (a carbon tax, for example) so emitters pay for every ton they emit; subsidize education, research, and vaccines so the creators of positive externalities get rewarded.

## 看懂外部性，才能看懂产品的真实成本

作为一个思维模型，外部性提醒你：**一件东西的标价，不等于它的真实成本。**一个汉堡的价格里没有算上环境的代价，一款免费应用的价格里没有算上你的注意力和隐私。学会追问"这笔交易的成本由谁承担、收益归谁所有"，你对商业世界的理解会深刻得多。

As a mental model, externalities remind you that the sticker price of a thing is not its true cost. The price of a hamburger doesn't include the environmental toll; the price of a free app doesn't include your attention and your privacy. Learn to ask who bears the costs and who captures the benefits of a transaction, and you'll understand the business world far more deeply.

从长远看，建立在把成本悄悄转嫁给社会之上的生意是脆弱的——社会迟早会把账算回来，无论是通过监管、诉讼还是声誉的崩塌。真正持久的生意，创造的价值远大于它留给世界的隐性账单。

In the long run, businesses built on quietly shifting costs onto society are fragile — sooner or later society sends the bill back, through regulation, lawsuits, or reputational collapse. Durable businesses create far more value than the hidden invoice they leave behind.
