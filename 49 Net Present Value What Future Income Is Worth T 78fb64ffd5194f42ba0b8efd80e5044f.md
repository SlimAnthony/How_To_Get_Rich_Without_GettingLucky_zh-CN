# **净现值：未来的收入在今天值多少钱**

## 明天的一块钱，不等于今天的一块钱

**Naval：** 净现值（Net Present Value，简称NPV）回答的是一个简单的问题：未来的一笔收入，放在今天究竟值多少钱？

Net Present Value (NPV) answers a simple question: How much is a stream of future income actually worth today?

明天的一块钱不等于今天的一块钱。今天的钱可以立刻拿去投资、产生回报，还不用承担通货膨胀和各种不确定性的风险。所以，要把未来的钱换算成今天的价值，就必须打一个折扣——这个折扣的比率，就叫贴现率（discount rate）。

A dollar tomorrow is not worth a dollar today. Money today can be invested immediately and earn a return, and it carries no inflation risk or uncertainty. So to convert future money into today's value, you have to apply a discount — and the rate of that discount is called the discount rate.

未来的收入越遥远、越不确定，折扣就应该打得越狠。一家稳定的老牌公司未来的现金流，可以用较低的贴现率；一家九死一生的初创公司许诺的未来收益，就必须用非常高的贴现率来折算。

The further out and the more uncertain the future income, the steeper the discount you should apply. The future cash flows of a stable, established company deserve a low discount rate; the promised future returns of a long-shot startup have to be discounted at a very high rate.

## 这就是投资者给公司估值的方法

净现值是金融世界的通用语言。当投资者给一家公司估值时，本质上就是在做这道计算题：预测这家公司未来所有年份能产生的现金流，然后用一个反映风险的贴现率，把它们全部折算回今天，加总起来。

NPV is the common language of the financial world. When investors value a company, they're essentially doing this calculation: project all the cash the company will generate in future years, then discount it all back to today at a rate that reflects the risk, and add it up.

这也解释了为什么有些暂时不赚钱的公司依然值很多钱——市场相信它未来的现金流巨大；也解释了为什么利率一变，所有资产的价格都会跟着变——贴现率变了，未来收入折算到今天的价值就全变了。

This explains why some companies that aren't yet profitable can still be worth a lot — the market believes their future cash flows are enormous. It also explains why every asset price moves when interest rates move — change the discount rate, and the present value of all future income changes with it.

## 用净现值思维做人生决策

对个人来说，净现值思维在你评估工作机会时尤其有用。一份高薪但没有成长的工作，和一份薪水较低、但附带股权和技能复利的工作，哪个更值钱？把两者未来十年的收入流折算到今天比一比，答案往往和直觉相反。

For individuals, NPV thinking is especially useful when you're weighing job offers. Which is worth more: a high-paying job with no growth, or a lower-paying one that comes with equity and compounding skills? Discount both income streams over the next ten years back to today and compare — the answer is often the opposite of your intuition.

**年轻时用一部分眼前收入，去换取学习、声誉和股权这些会复利增长的资产，往往是净现值最高的选择。**

Trading some current income while you're young for learning, reputation, and equity — assets that compound — is often the choice with the highest net present value.
